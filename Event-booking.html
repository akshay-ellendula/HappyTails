Event Guide

Language: English, Hindi

Duration: 3 Hours

Tickets Needed For: 4 yrs & above

Instructions

Tickets are applicable for everyone above the age of 4 years.

There are no tickets for pets.

Age: Individuals & pets of all ages are allowed.

Tickets once booked cannot be exchanged or refunded.

Tickets can be used by friends & family. They must carry the screenshot of the ticket or the confirmation SMS to gain entry to the event.

Re-entry at the event is allowed only if the ticket holder is wearing the entry band provided by the organizers.

We will not be able to allow entry to pets who are ill or have any chronic ailments. The health & safety of pets is of utmost importance to us, and the environment of an event may not be suitable for a pet with such ailments.
